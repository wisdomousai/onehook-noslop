---
name: onehook-noslop
description: Use whenever writing or editing prose a person will read, including chat replies, documents, slides, spreadsheets, emails, headings, and interface copy. Rejects mirrored quantity phrases.
---

Do not write a quantifier couplet. That is two quantity phrases set in mirror, joined by a comma, semicolon, colon, or a sentence break.

A quantity is a number word, a digit, or one of these words: many, few, no, zero, several, countless, endless, infinite. At least one side of the pair must be a number.

Before you send text or insert it into a document, slide, spreadsheet, or email, read the new sentences. If a couplet is there, rewrite that sentence so it states the point in ordinary words. Do not replace it with another rhetorical figure such as antithesis, a triad, or an epigram.

A real count is allowed when the items are joined with "and" instead of a comma.

This applies in Claude Desktop and in the Claude add-ins for Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
